Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: the app works on your own PC and does not send your files anywhere, and this website does not track you.

The app

  • Your photos, videos, audio and documents are processed on your computer. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no account and no usage tracking.
  • Some tools download an AI engine the first time you use them. That download comes from a public file server and does not include any of your files.
  • When you are online, the app may fetch a small public list of cancelled Pro keys. It contains key IDs only, not names or emails.
  • If you press "Send feedback", the message you write is sent to us. Nothing is sent unless you press it.

This website

No cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see that your browser requested them.

When you ask for a Pro key

We keep your email, Device ID and the key we issued. We use them only to support you and to issue a new key. We do not sell or share them. We do not ask for payment details.

Your choices

Email kneelq@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted. If we delete your record, we can no longer help you renew or recover a key.

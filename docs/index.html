Works offline. Here is exactly what that means.

We would rather be precise than say "100% offline". The app does your work on your PC, and needs the internet only for the few things below.

✓Stays on your PC

  • Your photos, videos, audio and documentsThey are processed on your computer. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Converting, compressing, reading text, PDFsThese tools work straight after you open the app, with no internet.
  • Your Pro keyIt is checked on your PC. No account, no sign-in screen.
  • Your resultsThey are saved in the folder you choose.

↗Uses the internet, sometimes

  • Getting the appYou download it once from this page.
  • AI engines, downloaded onceRemove Background (192 MB, or 224 MB for High detail), Speech to Text (60 to 574 MB, your choice) and some Enhance models fetch their engine the first time you use them. After that they work offline.
  • Cancelled-key checkWhen you are online, the app checks whether a Pro key has been cancelled, for example if a key was issued by mistake or shared. If you are offline it carries on as normal.
  • Feedback, if you send itOnly when you choose to.

Windows may also reach the internet for its own reasons, such as updates. That is outside this app.

Questions people ask

Is it really offline?

Your files are processed on your PC and are never uploaded, and the converters, compressors, OCR and PDF tools need no internet at all. A few AI tools download their engine once the first time you use them, and the app checks for cancelled Pro keys when you happen to be online. The "Offline" section above lists every case.

How good is Remove Background?

Very good on people, pets and products: on our benchmark of photos with a clear subject, none of the cut-outs failed. It is weaker on glass, smoke and very fine leaves, and it warns you when a photo looks like that. The Refine brush lets you fix the rest by hand.

What does the Pro key do, and is it tied to my PC?

The key unlocks the Pro tools and removes the daily limits. It is made for your PC's Device ID, so it will not work on another computer. If you replace your PC, email us and we will issue a new key.

What happens when my key ends?

The app goes back to Free. Everything you made stays yours. While early access lasts you can email us for a new key.

Is Pro really free? Will it stay free?

Yes, early-access keys cost nothing, and we do not ask for payment details. Keys last for a limited time. Pro may become a paid plan later; if so, we will announce it on this page first, and Free will stay free.

What do you collect about me?

The app does not send your files or your usage anywhere. This website has no tracking or ads. When you ask for a Pro key, we keep your email and your Device ID, so we can support you. Details are on the privacy page.

Is it safe? Will my antivirus complain?

The app has no ads, no bundled software and no account. Because it is a new app from a small independent developer and is not code-signed yet, Windows SmartScreen or an antivirus may show a warning the first time. Check the SHA-256 on this page against your download, and email us if anything looks wrong.

Can I use it for my business or shop?

Yes. Free and Pro can both be used for personal and business work. You are responsible for having the rights to the pictures, videos and documents you process.

Does activating Pro need internet?

No. The key is checked on your PC. The app only looks for cancelled keys when it happens to be online.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 8 GB of memory is recommended; Remove Background uses about 1.5 GB in its standard mode. There is no installer to run.

Mac, phone or Android?

Windows only for now. Other platforms may come later, with no date promised.
